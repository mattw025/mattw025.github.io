## National University of Singapore

**Undergraduates:** Supervisions and tutorials on writing and critical thinking, and historical source criticism for first-year undergraduates. Lectures and seminar discussions on oral-history methodology.

## University of Cambridge

**Undergraduates:** Seminar classes on temporality and gender for final-year history undergraduates.

**Postgraduates:** Seminar classes and workshops on research conceptualisation and presentation.

## University of Hull

**Graduate interns:** Bespoke training tailored to interns' self-identified learning and development goals. Topics included digital graphics, digital storytelling and data visualisation, productivity tools, research tools and reference management, and career development.
