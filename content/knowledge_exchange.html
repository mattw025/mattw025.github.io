Green Toolkit for a New Space Economy

I co-led a university consortium project to incorporate societal and cultural impacts into the evaluation of UK outer-space activities. Funded by the UK Space Agency (OW131743BZ14S), the project aimed to develop a toolkit for UK space-sector SMEs to assess the effects of their activities on the environment and human society.

The project brought together collaborators in law, physics, cybersecurity, and user design from the Universities of Edinburgh, Southampton, and York St John, working in partnership with a UK space SME. The Green Toolkit was intended to help businesses navigate the regulatory landscape and reduce adverse social, cultural, and environmental impacts.

For this work, I was awarded the University of Hull’s Best Knowledge Exchange Postdoc prize in 2022.

Beyond the Page

In 2025, I helped oversee Beyond the Page, a collaborative oral-history and exhibition project involving the NUS Department of History and the NUS History Society. Students interviewed 12 History alumni and researched the Department’s archives, producing an exhibition for NUS Central Library.

Walking Through a Songline

I convened the Singapore iteration of Walking Through a Songline, an immersive digital exhibition created by the National Museum of Australia and Mosster Studio. The Singapore presentation was jointly organised by the NUS Department of History and the National Museum of Australia, with additional content that I curated for its presentation at NUS Central Library in 2025–26. I also took part in an interdisciplinary public discussion about evidence and knowledge across history, physics, museum practice, and Indigenous knowledge systems.

Decolonising UK History Provision in Schools

I helped reformulate history curricula with Pearson Edexcel and AQA, including work on subject specifications and the review of GCSE history textbooks. I have also co-authored evidence and provided research expertise to the UK Parliament.
